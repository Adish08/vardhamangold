The Filtration Process

Triple-Filtered vs. Refined Oils

Standard refined oils undergo heavy chemical neutralization, bleaching, and high-heat deodorization, which strips away natural nutrients. Our Triple-Filtration process physical cleans the oil through micro-mesh layers, retaining natural Vitamin E, aroma, and heart-healthy monounsaturated fats.

Zero Additives

Strictly free from mineral oils, argemone oil, preservatives, or artificial colouring agents.

Farmer Sourced

Direct procurement from local farmers in Neemuch and surrounding Malwa farm belts.
