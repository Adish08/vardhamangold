Brand Story & Heritage

Manufacturing Ethics

How We Process Peanut Oil

Step 1

Optical Seed Sorting

Peanut seeds are screened through optical grain sorters, removing discoloured, damaged, or small seeds. Only large-kernel, high-oil seeds proceed to crushing.

Step 2

Cold Crushing

The seeds are cold-pressed mechanically at low temperatures (below 50°C). This low-temperature extraction ensures that critical vitamins and omega fatty acids do not burn away.

Step 3

Triple Filtration

The raw oil is pumped through a sequential series of three physical micro-mesh filters. This removes seed sediments while leaving natural color, aroma, and nutrients intact.

Sourcing the Malwa Belt

Cultivated with Indian Farmers

Neemuch is famous for its agricultural markets. By buying raw peanut (Mungphali) crops directly from local farmers during harvest season, we support agrarian families while locking in raw materials at peak freshness.
